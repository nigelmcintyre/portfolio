Common questions

What if I already have a website?

That is fine. I can rebuild it and move it across, keeping your domain and anything worth keeping. There is no gap where your site is offline.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your business's name and stays yours. If you ever move to someone else, you take it with you.

How long does it take?

Usually two to three weeks from our first chat, depending on how quickly you can get me your photos and details.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, opening hours, a list of your services or menu, and whatever photos you have. If your photos are not great, I will tell you straight and we will sort it.

Can I cancel?

Any time, with a month's notice. There is no long contract to sign and no exit fee.
